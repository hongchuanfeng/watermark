About ChuanDao AI

Empowering enterprise digital transformation with code and AI technology

Company Overview

Since its establishment in 2019, ChuanDao AI has consistently anchored its core positioning of "using technology to solve business pain points and AI to activate growth potential." From a startup team focused on software development, we have gradually grown into a digital solution service provider covering 18 core cities nationwide and serving over 300 enterprise clients. We understand that in the era of deep integration of AI and industry, "good software" is not just a collection of code, but a "digital engine" that fits business logic and carries strategic goals. Therefore, over the past five years, we have always been driven by the dual wheels of "technical depth + demand insight," continuously breaking through in the field of software development and helping enterprises cross the "last mile" of digital transformation.

Technical Strength & Team Building

ChuanDao AI has built a core team of "practical + innovative" members, with over 120 current members, of which technical R&D personnel account for more than 70%, and 85% have more than 6 years of software development experience. The core team members all come from major internet companies and leading technology enterprises, having led the development of million-user scale projects such as financial risk control systems, intelligent manufacturing management platforms, and new retail data middle platforms.

Core Business & Service System

Customized Software Development

Targeting enterprise personalized needs, providing full-process services from requirement research, architecture design, code development to testing and launch, covering three major directions: enterprise management systems (ERP/CRM/OA), industry-specific software, and mobile applications.

AI-Powered Solutions

Deeply integrating AI technology with industry scenarios, developing software systems with intelligent decision-making and automated processing capabilities, such as AI risk control audit systems, AI intelligent recommendation systems, etc.

Full-Cycle Technical Operations Service

Breaking the traditional model of "development as the end point," providing customers with 1-3 years of exclusive operation and maintenance services, equipped with 7×24-hour technical support team, with an average response time of no more than 30 minutes.

Social Responsibility & Brand Philosophy

ChuanDao AI has always practiced the value of "technology for good," closely integrating enterprise development with social value. In 2021, we launched the "AI Enterprise Assistance Public Welfare Program," providing free basic management software development and digital consulting services to 15 small and medium-sized enterprises; in 2023, we jointly launched the "ChuanDao AI Technology Training Camp" with universities, training over 500 software development talents with practical capabilities.

Looking to the Future

ChuanDao AI will continue to build on "software development" as the foundation and "AI technology innovation" as the breakthrough point, planning to launch an "AI lightweight management suite" for small and medium-sized enterprises in 2025 to reduce digital transformation costs; at the same time, expanding into new tracks such as industrial software and cross-border e-commerce systems, creating more industry benchmark projects.
